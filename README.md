# Kemet: Blood and Sand — BGA prototype

This is a playable, server-validated prototype with artwork converted from the PDFs in `img/`.
It follows the core turn structure in the [Blood and Sand rulebook](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/af/f0/59-kemet-blood-and-sand-rulebook.pdf). A local copy is `rules-reference.pdf`.

## Implemented

- Two to five players, printed-board city layouts and three districts per active city, starting with two five-unit troops, pyramids, prayer points, two DI cards and eight battle cards. As in the DEF rulebook, setup ends with each player choosing a Level 1 Power tile of one of their pyramid colors, in reverse turn order.
- Five turns per player in each Day Phase. Each turn plays one colored Action token, plus optionally the silver token (Act of God, once per Round) and the gold token (a tile's Golden Action, once per Round). Action spaces are used once per day and the three-tier equilibrium rule is enforced.
- Pray, buy a power, raise a pyramid, recruit, and move (including mapped land routes, harbor travel, pyramid-to-obelisk teleportation, and extra land movement from a power).
- Battle war council with private played and discarded card choices, optional DI card, strength, tie to defender, casualties, veteran rewards, battle VP, and loser/winner aftermath choices.
- Temple and level-four pyramid temporary VP, permanent VP from battle and powers, night offerings, temple income, veteran conversion, action reset, and the start-of-turn nine-VP victory condition.

## Prototype boundaries

The clickable board now places every active district and shared zone over the matching printed artwork. The six printed city sites follow the rulebook's two-to-five-player configurations; storm-covered and out-of-play districts are marked grey. Land links are traced from visible territory boundaries, and the harbor rules allow departure from any harbor to a trading harbor or the military harbor outside your own city. The current map still uses a reduced route graph: it models one or two waypoints per move, does not model every harbor and wall nuance, and assigns starting cities automatically. The power market uses the 64 printed Power tiles with the player-count rules (2 players: 2 colors, 26 tiles, no Act of God and one copy of each duplicate; 3–4 players: 3 colors, 48 tiles; 5 players: 4 colors, 64 tiles). Tile cost equals its level, and a player cannot buy a second tile with the same name. Every Power tile is implemented. Passive and "When acquired" effects are data-driven modifiers (`mods` in `PrototypeRules::tiles()`). Creatures start in the owner's supply, are tied to a Troop in one of their Districts during their turn, and add their move/battle bonuses (Khnum's Sphinx, Griffin Sphinx, Phoenix and Deep Desert Snake have their special rules). Golden Actions (Twin Ceremony, Forced March, Divine Will, Surprise Attack) use the gold Action token on the Divine Power space; Act of God gives the silver token. Spy, Divine Wound, Vision, Hand of God, Reinforcement Prayer and Local Recruitment have their own steps. The Wrryt Storm and Shetyw Barrier Special Battle cards are not printed in the supplied PDFs: they are drawn with their Power tile art and use provisional values (flagged in their tooltips) until the real card values are supplied. As in the rulebook setup, the tile colors are chosen at random and every tile starts in the market. The DI deck is a reduced set with text effects; its cards are prototype stand-ins. Recruit currently places units in one district per action. Action spaces follow the printed Individual board (top: Move, Recruit; middle: Raise, Move, Pray; bottom: Pray and the Buy a Power Tile spaces); the gold Action token sits on the Divine Power space.

Edit `modules/php/PrototypeRules.php` to change the graph and catalog. The PHP game state lives in `modules/php/CoreGame.php`, with state actions in `modules/php/States/`. The interface is `modules/js/Game.js` and `kemetbloodandsands.css`. Restart a BGA table after deploying the new `dbmodel.sql` schema.

## Artwork

The interface uses compressed images derived from the supplied PDFs: the game board front as the interactive map, the five player board fronts as each player's action board (`img/player-boards.jpg`), and the punchboard Power tile faces (`img/power-tiles.jpg`, all 64 tiles). The supplied Player Aid provides five matching Divine Intervention card faces (`img/di-cards.jpg`); the punchboard provides transparent Acquire emblems (`img/acquire-tokens.png`) aligned to the Buy spaces on the Individual boards. A silver Action token illustration and a Battle card example are cropped from the rulebook (`img/silver-token.png`, `img/battle-sample.jpg`). `py -3 tools/build-art.py` rebuilds the sprites from the PDFs (needs Pillow and PyMuPDF). These files are under 1 MB each.

The supplied PDFs do not include standalone faces for all eight Battle cards, a complete Divine Intervention card face sheet, colored or gold Action token faces, troop or creature miniature sprites, or pyramid model artwork. The Battle cards share the rulebook's battle illustration as prototype art, with their individual values overlaid. The unmatched prototype Reinforcement DI card remains a CSS placeholder so it is not confused with the printed Tactical Support card, which has a different effect. Colored Action tokens remain plain colored discs like the physical pieces; the gold token reuses the silver illustration with a gold tint. Each Power tile in the catalog points at its own face in `img/power-tiles.jpg` (`art` index), shown in the market, the player panels and the tile tooltips. The board back and player board backs remain reference material rather than gameplay assets.

## BGA interface

The active choice is announced by BGA's action bar. The board and individual boards highlight selectable components. Pyramid colors and levels, troop counts, Golden Actions, veteran spending, and Destiny positions are chosen through graphical pieces at their relevant board locations. Confirm and cancel actions remain in the action bar. Map markers and action spaces have larger touch targets, keyboard focus outlines, and static highlights. Player-number badges and default color symbols distinguish pieces without relying on hue alone. The client reports failed requests and respects reduced-motion settings. Studio browser playtesting, mobile layout review, complete endgame scoring presentation, and the remaining prototype rules listed above still need validation before claiming release readiness.
